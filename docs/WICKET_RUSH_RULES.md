# Crickopoly: Wicket Rush

A Uno-style cricket card game. Real cricketers are the cards. Empty your hand first to win.

## The 30-second explanation

> Match the **current role** or play any **all-rounder** (wild card). Every card does a cricket action: batsmen score runs and force the next player to bowl, bowlers give a rival a wicket and skip them, wicketkeepers steal a card from a rival, and all-rounders reverse direction and let you play again. First manager to empty their hand wins.

## Setup

- 2–6 managers, human or AI.
- Each manager is dealt **7 real cricketer cards** (you can choose 5, 7 or 9 in the lobby).
- Remaining cricketers form the draw pile.
- Direction starts clockwise.

## Playing a turn

1. Look at the **current role** (the role of the last card played).
2. Play a card with that role, or any **all-rounder** (wild).
3. If you cannot play, draw **2 cards** and your turn ends.
4. Before the first card, any role is legal.

## Card effects

| Card role | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 🏏 **Batsman** | Add `10 + BAT/5` runs. Next player must play a **bowler or all-rounder**. |
| 🎯 **Bowler** | Give the next player **+1 wicket** and **skip their turn**. |
| 🧤 **Wicketkeeper** | Pick a rival: **steal their highest-BAT card** and make them draw 1. |
| ⭐ **All-rounder** | Wild. +5 runs, **reverse direction**, and **play again**. |

## Winning

First manager to empty their hand wins immediately. Runs and wickets are just bragging rights shown on the score strip:

> `score = runs − wickets × 5`

## Why it is not luck

You choose **which card to play**, **who to steal from**, and **when to draw**. Holding the right all-rounder or wicketkeeper at the right time is a decision, not a dice roll.
